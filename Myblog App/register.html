2. Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are fundamental to our architecture:

  • Information We Collect: We collect your name, email address, profile photo (if provided), and authored blog posts.
  • Password Security: Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption with automated salting.
